What's permanent

  • No owner, no upgrade: Name, symbol, image URI, mode, and fee % are locked forever once deployed.
  • Fixed 1B supply: Every coin starts at a 10 ETH FDV with 100% of supply locked in its Uniswap v4 pool.
  • 10-minute anti-sniper toll: Right after launch, buys and sells pay an 80% fee that decays linearly down to your normal fee over 10 minutes.
  • Pinto yield waits for $1: Trade fees buy Pinto into the Silo immediately; Silo yield mints once Pinto is above peg.
