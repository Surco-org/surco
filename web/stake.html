How it works

  • Linear 2–365 day decay: Weight = Chirp × remainingDays / 365, decaying daily with zero rounding drift.
  • Permanent Max-Lock: Pins weight at 100% of locked Chirp until you toggle it off (starting a 365-day countdown).
  • Next-day activation: New locks, additions, and coin switches activate at 00:00 UTC tomorrow — no flash-staking or sunrise-hopping.
  • Always withdrawable: VeChirp holds your Chirp directly with zero external dependency on Pinto or the Collective Silo.
