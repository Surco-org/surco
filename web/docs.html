Flagship coin

Chirp (CHIRP)

A fixed-supply coin on Base sold from a one-sided book and backed directly by Pinto in the Silo.

  • Fixed supply: 1,000,000,000 CHIRP (18 decimals) minted once on 2026-11-17. There is no mint function.
  • One-sided Uniswap v4 book: price opened at 0.01 Pinto and rises along the curve as people buy; it cannot drop below the opening price.
  • Instant Silo backing: every Pinto paid on a buy is deposited into Pinto's Silo inside the same transaction. Sells withdraw Pinto straight from the Silo right away — including germinating deposits — so sellers never wait.
  • 1% fee each way: buys and sells pay a 1% fee. Above $1, Silo yield (Earned Pinto) buys Chirp from the book and burns it permanently. Below $1, accumulated sell-fee Pinto can be sown in Pinto's Field only at maximum temperature; when Pods repay and are harvested, the Pinto returns to Silo backing and buys Chirp to burn.
  • Public upkeep: Claim yield & burn, Sow the pile, and Harvest are open buttons anyone can call. When Chirp sows, it names the Surco team wallet 0x0dB1…08bf as Pinto's referrer (any bonus Pods come from Pinto's referral program, never from Chirp's backing).
The backing layer

Pinto & the Silo

Pinto is a credit-based stablecoin on Base aiming for $1 across hourly Seasons. Every coin on Surco — both Chirp and every coin created on the Launcher — uses Pinto's Silo as its on-chain backing vault.

When Pinto is above $1

Pinto mints new Pinto each Season and splits it between the Field's Pod Line and Silo depositors. Surco vaults earn Earned Pinto proportional to their Stalk and convert that yield into coin buybacks (Burn vaults and Collective Silo) or growing redemption value (Redeem vaults).

When Pinto is below $1

Pinto stops minting for Silo depositors and borrows via the Field instead. While below peg, Surco vaults keep accumulating Pinto from trade fees and holding their Silo deposits (growing Stalk via Seeds), but buyback yield waits until Pinto trades back above $1.

Read the full plain-language guide on /pinto or inspect Pinto's own Disclosures.

Coin Launcher

Launch, trade, and earn on Base

Anyone can launch a coin in one transaction whose trade fees continuously buy Pinto into a permanent Silo vault.

What happens in a single launch transaction

  1. Token deployed: an immutable ERC-20 with a fixed 1,000,000,000 supply (18 decimals, zero mint function) and on-chain imageUrl(), metadataUri(), and contractURI() (ERC-7572) metadata for wallets and Base token listings.
  2. Vault deployed: a dedicated, ownerless BurnVault or RedeemVault linked permanently to the coin.
  3. Locked Uniswap v4 pool: 100% of supply is deposited single-sided into a COIN/WETH Uniswap v4 pool starting at a 10 ETH fully diluted value. Liquidity is locked in the hook forever and cannot be removed.
  4. Optional creator first buy: any ETH sent above the launch fee buys the coin inside the launch transaction at the normal trade fee, before the anti-sniper toll starts.

Trading & seeding

  • Direct v4 swaps on /coin: buy with native ETH (zero approvals via Universal Router) or WETH, and sell to ETH or WETH using exact-amount Permit2 approvals. Because pools live directly on Base Uniswap v4, coins also route automatically on Base App and index on DexScreener / GeckoTerminal.
  • Voluntary seed(): anyone can transfer Pinto directly to any vault and call seed() to book it straight into that vault's Silo principal.
Vault modes

Burn mode vs. Redeem mode

Chosen once at launch and permanent forever.

Burn mode (default)

Silo Pinto principal is locked forever and can never be withdrawn. When Pinto trades above $1, Silo yield (buyback()) buys the coin from its locked Uniswap v4 pool — guarded by the hook's 10-minute tick EMA — and permanently burns 100% of the coins bought. Supply only ever falls.

Redeem mode

Silo Pinto backs the coin directly as a redeemable floor. Any holder can call redeem() anytime to burn coins for their exact pro-rata share of the vault's Silo Pinto (coins × siloPinto / totalSupply), minus a 1% fee that stays inside the vault and raises backing per remaining coin.

Collective Silo & veChirp

Lock Chirp, direct yield, earn coins

Live on Base mainnet at /stake.

Every coin launched on the real Surco factory pays a flat 0.0001 ETH launch fee routed straight (as WETH) into an immutable shared Collective Pinto Silo (CollectiveSilo), alongside any voluntary seed() Pinto. Like a Burn vault, Collective Silo principal is never withdrawn.

How veChirp NFTs work

  • Minimal ERC-721 lock: lock any amount of CHIRP in VeChirp to mint a transferable veChirp NFT with live on-chain SVG metadata.
  • Fixed term (2–365 UTC days): voting weight equals CHIRP × remainingDays / 365, decaying cleanly by 1 day every UTC midnight with zero rounding drift.
  • Permanent Max-Lock toggle: pin your weight at 100% of locked CHIRP indefinitely. Whenever you toggle Permanent off, a standard 365-day countdown begins at the next UTC midnight.
  • One coin per NFT, next-day activation: each veChirp NFT directs its weight to one factory-launched coin. New locks, additions, extensions, permanent toggles, and coin switches take effect at 00:00 UTC the following day — making flash-staking and sunrise-hopping impossible.
  • 100% of bought coins to lockers: when Pinto is above $1, plant() allocates daily Collective Silo yield across all backed coins by their veChirp weight (days with zero staked Chirp roll yield into permanent principal). Once a UTC day ends, distribute() swaps each coin's Pinto share through its locked v4 pool (same EMA PriceGuard, 0.5% Pinto tip), and veChirp NFT owners call claim() to receive 100% of the coins bought for their share.
  • Always withdrawable: VeChirp holds locked CHIRP directly with zero external dependency on Pinto or CollectiveSilo. Once a lock's UTC end day passes, your CHIRP is always withdrawable via withdraw(), and any past unclaimed coin rewards remain claimable even after burning the NFT.
Automation & safety

Permissionless keepers & price guards

  • Anyone can turn vaults over: split(), convert(), buyback(), claim(), seed(), plant(), and distribute() have no access control. Caller tips are fixed at 0.5% on convert() (in WETH) and on buyback() / distribute() (in Pinto).
  • Subsidized daily keeper: Surco operates a daily keeper on Base that converts vaults once fees reach 0.0005 WETH, books seeded Pinto, and runs at-peg buybacks and Collective Silo distributions.
  • Three-layer sandwich protection: every swap route is checked on-chain against (1) Uniswap v3's 15-minute TWAP on WETH/cbETH (±0.5%), (2) Basin's Pump EMA on cbETH/Pinto (±0.5%), and (3) the launcher hook's own 10-minute tick EMA on each COIN/WETH pool (±5% drift cap, ±3% min-out tolerance). Manipulated blocks revert cleanly instead of losing value.

Risks & limits

Read before you trade or lock

  • Pinto peg & upgradability: all Surco backing sits in Pinto's Silo. Pinto is a credit-based stablecoin that can trade below $1 for extended periods — while below $1, Silo deposits earn no new Pinto. Pinto's contract can also be upgraded by a 5-of-9 multisig (Pinto Upgradability).
  • Immutable contracts: Surco contracts have no owner, no pause, and no upgrade path. If an upstream dependency breaks or an undiscovered bug exists, deployed contracts cannot be patched in place.
  • Permissionless launcher: anyone can launch a coin on the factory. Listing on the coins directory is automatic and not an endorsement. Always verify the contract address, fee %, and vault mode (Burn vs Redeem) before trading.
  • veChirp lock commitment: locked CHIRP cannot be withdrawn early before its UTC end day. Turning off Permanent Max-Lock starts a 365-day countdown before withdrawal is possible.
  • Market risk: Chirp, Pinto, and every coin launched on Surco can lose value. Nothing on this site is financial, legal, or tax advice.
