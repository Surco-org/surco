The perpetual flywheel

No admin keys. Just a self-feeding Pinto loop.

No fake roadmaps and no upgradeable contracts. Every coin launched on Surco is wired into an automated on-chain loop that turns trade volume into permanent Pinto Silo backing.

STEP 01

Mint & trade on Base

One transaction mints a fixed-supply coin with a locked Uniswap v4 pool. Every buy and sell captures an immutable on-chain swap fee.

Locked v4 pool
STEP 02

Fees buy Pinto into the Silo

Captured WETH fees automatically market-buy Pinto and deposit it into Pinto's Silo — growing both the coin's vault and the Collective Silo.

Permanent backing
STEP 03

Silo yield buys the coin back

When Pinto earns Silo yield, anyone can trigger buybacks — burning supply (or deepening redeem backing), while veChirp directs Collective Silo yield.

Buyback & burn
STEP 04

Compounds forever

Every buyback drives fresh swaps and fresh fees back into Pinto's Silo. Principal never leaves — the flywheel keeps spinning with zero admin control.
