About

The words

One line

Chirp is a coin on Base backed by Pinto in the silo. Silo yield buys Chirp back and burns it. No owner, no pause, no upgrade.

A paragraph

Chirp is a token on Base, sold from a one-sided book at surcofi.com. Every Pinto paid for Chirp is deposited in Pinto's silo in the same trade, and a sell pays Pinto back from it right away. The price opened at 0.01 Pinto and rises as people buy; it cannot go below the open. The silo's yield buys Chirp back and burns it. One billion Chirp were minted once, the fee is 1% each way, and the contracts have no owner, no pause and no upgrade.
